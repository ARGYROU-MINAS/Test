Privacy Policy for Hello World

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy applies to the Android app Hello World (package name eu.argyrouminas.test).

Information we collect

The app displays a fixed “Hello World!” message. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal information or device data. It does not require an account or request device permissions.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

The app contains no analytics or advertising services. It does not share information with the developer or third parties.

Data retention

The app does not collect data, so it has no user data to retain or delete.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date before those changes take effect.
